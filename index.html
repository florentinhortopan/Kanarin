Red — Alert

What it is

One signal, one action.

The room goes still without looking different. A kitchen after frying, a closed hour after guests, a walk from the door — nothing announces the air. Kanarin is a pebble-sized canary for two lives: magnet on the fridge, bead on the wrist.

Not a dashboard

No charts, no ppm on the object, no app required. Fridge magnet or wearable. The phone can stay in another room.

Not a smoke alarm

When the air needs exchanging, the whole bead turns red and plays a short chirp. Then it waits. Open a window.

Bring nature back

Light

Same bird, same fridge. Only the glow changes. Tap a state to put it on the door.

The other home

Same bird, closer.

A slim bone strap. The canary sits on a wrist instead of across a kitchen. Light still answers one question — idle, caution, or alert. When the air needs exchanging, the bird turns red and the chirp is felt.

  • Jewelry, not a tracker.
  • Felt on the skin, not shouted across a room.

Amber — Caution

Language

Chirps

Heard preview: canary gestures around 2–6 kHz. Felt on the bead: the same envelopes, quieter. Idle, Caution, and Breathe stay silent. Play lives on the edges — when the air changes, not every sample.

Kitchen does not get a speaker. Heard is a preview so you can learn the five gestures. On the bead they are felt.
